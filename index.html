How ordering works

Checkout stays on rarecharacterwhiskey.com. Behind it, AccelPay routes every order to a licensed retailer in the buyer's state.

STEP 1

Enter your address

We check which licensed retailers can sell to you, and show their price and stock for the bottle you picked.

STEP 2

Pick shipping or delivery

Ship from a retailer in your state, or choose same-day delivery when a stocking retailer is local.

STEP 3

Pour

The retailer fulfills the order. An adult signature (21+) is required at the door.

Instant delivery partners: Gopuff, about 30 minutes DoorDash from local retailers

Reordering is built in

Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.

What this demo shows

  • Rare Character has no online store today. This is rarecharacterwhiskey.com with AccelPay checkout added, so bottles sell on your own domain. It works on your Squarespace site as it is.
  • Every order is sold and fulfilled by a licensed retailer in the buyer's state, from a network of 3,500+ licensed retail locations reaching 40+ states.
  • Prices are what Liquor Barn (IL) and Bottle Republic (NJ) charge for these bottles today. Two bottles show as connected to retailers at launch. Licensed retailers set the final retail price.
  • Instant delivery runs through Gopuff (about 30 minutes) and DoorDash from local retailers, wherever a bottle is stocked nearby.
  • Launch takes about one week, with no per-order charge to Rare Character.
  • Reordering is built in. We track depletions at each retailer and flag reorders with suggested POs when stock runs low. For brands distributed by Southern Glazer's, we're setting up a pilot to send those reorders straight into Proof.
  • Brands that switch their white-label store to our plugins (the AccelPay or LiquidCommerce cart, or our native Shopify integration) automatically receive advertising credit on ReserveBar.com and Minibar.
  • Rare Character is already listed on ReserveBar.com.
  • "Demo route" retailers, fees and tax are illustrative. No order is ever placed.
